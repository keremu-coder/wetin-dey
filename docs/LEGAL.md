## Disclaimer

Naija Archive na civic record of anonymous reports. We no be court, police, or government agency. Reports for this site no be independently verified. If you dey under threat, stop, leave this site, and seek local help wey you trust.

## Privacy

We no collect phone number, email, or name for anonymous reporting. Session na one signed HTTP-only cookie with a random id. We no store IP addresses for anonymous activity. Photos: EXIF and GPS metadata dey stripped on your device and again on the server. Clearing cookies go lose the link between this browser and “my reports”. Do not upload pictures wey show faces, house numbers, or anything wey fit identify you.

## Takedown

Reports no dey delete. URLs remain. If you believe a report is defamatory, unlawful, or puts someone at immediate risk, use the Dispute control on the report page or write through a channel we publish later. Dispute go label the report. E no go erase am. Reviewed / Not reviewed na a separate moderator flag; e no be the same thing as the status badge.
