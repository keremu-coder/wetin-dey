# App Store & Play Store Screenshot Checklist

When you are ready to submit, you will need to take screenshots on actual devices (or emulators).

## Required Sizes
Apple requires two specific sizes, and will scale the larger one down:
- **6.7" (iPhone 15 Pro Max)** — 1290 x 2796 pixels (REQUIRED)
- **6.5" (iPhone 11 Pro Max / XS Max)** — 1242 x 2688 pixels
- **12.9" iPad Pro** — 2048 x 2732 pixels (only if you support iPad)

Google Play requires at minimum:
- **Phone** — 16:9 or 9:16 ratio, minimum 1080 x 1920 pixels (up to 8 screenshots)

## What to Capture (The 5 Screens)

1. **The Hero (Homepage)**
   - Incognito mode (no chats).
   - Show: "Where Naija dey as one" hero + trust markers + recent record.
   - Caption: "Your country on the internet."

2. **The Civic Record (/talk)**
   - Show: The "Community's memory" header and a mix of reported and resolved reports.
   - Caption: "Wetin we don settle no dey disappear."

3. **The Report Detail (Evidence & AI)**
   - Tap into a report.
   - Show: The clean "Open source link" button, the AI-generated summary, and the comments.
   - Caption: "Evidence-backed reports, translated for everyone."

4. **The Gathering (Spaces/Chat)**
   - Open a chat room.
   - Show: The message bubbles, a voice note, and the "Reply/Report/Block" menu open.
   - Caption: "Your people, your space, no shaking."

5. **Radar (Safety)**
   - Open `/radar`.
   - Show: The "Need Help" or "Police Stop" interface.
   - Caption: "Safety in your pocket. Live location for your Circle."

## Preparation for Device Testing
- Use the "Naija Market" or "Gamers Lounge" seeded spaces.
- Post 2-3 messages from a second account so the chat doesn't look empty.
- Ensure a report with a public link (e.g., the PHED news article) is at the top of the feed.
